## 📽️ DETAILED CINEMATIC SCRIPTS

### ENDING 1: "THE RESCUE" (Achievement: Shepherd)
*Condition: Rescued 5–6 Crew Members | Reactor Scrammed | Data Lost in Reactor Chamber*

**[VISUAL]**  
*Hermes IV* extraction shuttle launches from Aruna pad. Exhaust plumes illuminate the jagged, shadow-draped peaks of Shackleton Crater. Through the shuttle viewport, Aruna Base flickers into blackness as emergency power banks finally collapse. 

In the passenger bay, Captain Sarah Chen, Dr. Patel, Engineer Tanaka, and the awakened specialists Marcus Vance and Elena Rostova sit strapped into crash couches. They are breathing oxygen from umbilical lines. No one speaks. Their faces are lit only by the green glow of vitals monitors.

**[VOICEOVER: DR. AIDEN OKAFOR]**  
"We left the data behind. Five petabytes of telemetry, spectral readings of the ancient structures, the evidence that we aren't alone... burned to slag in the reactor chamber. NASA asked me if it was worth sacrificing the scientific discovery of the millennium. I looked back at the six people breathing in my cabin. People who will see their children again. People who will feel sunlight on Earth. That is my answer. That will always be my answer."

**[EPILOGUE]**  
Sarah Chen resigns her commission six months later, publishing *The Cost of Shackleton*, advocating for strict ethical limits on deep space commercial exploration. The crater site is declared an international cemetery and off-limits sanctuary. Humanity remains safe... but unaware of what watches from the dark.

### ENDING 2: "THE DISCOVERY" (Achievement: Pioneer)
*Condition: Rescued 3–4 Crew Members | Meridian Data Drive Secured | Entity Remains Active*

**[VISUAL]**  
The shuttle accelerates into trans-Earth trajectory. On the flight deck console rests Dr. Murthy's glowing optical drive, encased in magnetic insulation. 

Earth hangs in the viewport like a blue marble. Down in the medical compartment, Dr. Murthy stares at the telemetry stream, tears welling in his eyes. Two empty crash couches with emergency thermal blankets signify the losses of Patel and Vance.

**[VOICEOVER: DR. JAMES MURTHY]**  
"They called us mad. But here it is: the 432-hertz sequence. It’s an interstellar navigation catalog. It maps nineteen exoplanets with harmonic resonances matching the structures under the Moon. Humanity was born inside an incubator. Today, we cracked the shell. Those we lost... their names will be etched onto the first starships we launch."

**[EPILOGUE]**  
The discovery of the Meridian Signal sparks a global scientific renaissance. Space budgets increase by 400%. The UN establishes the Interstellar Exploration Directorate. Yet, back at the South Pole of the Moon, the crystalline entity continues to circle the empty halls of Aruna Base, vibrating into the lunar bedrock... waiting for humanity’s return.

### ENDING 3: "THE SACRIFICE" (Achievement: The Last Guardian)
*Condition: Manual Reactor Overload Triggered by Okafor | Crew & Data Escaped on Hermes IV*

**[VISUAL]**  
Inside the Tokamak chamber, Dr. Aiden Okafor stands on the vibrating gantry. Coolant alarms wail in deafening polyphony. The manual dog-latch of the scram valve is seized in his hands.

Through the observation port, the shuttle *Hermes IV* ignites its main engines and ascends into the black sky. On the central HUD, a radio transmission from Sarah Chen plays in static bursts.

**[AUDIO: SARAH CHEN (RADIO CRACKLE)]**  
"Aiden! The telemetry is locked! We're airborne! Aiden, please... can you hear me? ...Thank you. We will tell them. We will tell everyone what you did."

**[VISUAL]**  
Okafor looks up. Hovering ten meters above the plasma containment field, the Entity unfolds into a breathtaking kaleidoscope of violet, prismatic light. It doesn't strike. It lowers toward him, matching his heartbeat. 
Okafor smiles faintly inside his helmet, exhaling his final breath as white Cherenkov light engulfs the screen.

**[EPILOGUE]**  
Aiden Okafor Base is established ten years later at the Shackleton Rim. A three-meter titanium monument stands in the vacuum where Aruna’s reactor once lay, bearing the inscription: *“He silenced the storm so that others could speak.”*

### ENDING 4: "COMMUNICATION" (Achievement: First Contact)
*Condition: Tuned Pings to 432 Hz Harmonic | Entity Pacified | Base Stabilized | Data Preserved*

**[VISUAL]**  
The central reactor hall is quiet. The red warning klaxons have powered down. Floating at the center of the chamber, the Entity pulses in rhythmic synchrony with Okafor's suit acoustic ping.

Dr. Murthy and Captain Chen stand behind Okafor on the catwalk. The Entity emits a sequence of three crystalline chimes. Okafor taps his keypad, playing back the inverted third harmonic. A resonant ripple of light washes over the walls, restoring emergency power to the habitat grids. The station comes alive with warm amber illumination.

**[VOICEOVER: DR. AIDEN OKAFOR]**  
"We came looking for monsters in the dark. We found a teacher. The entity wasn't an invader; it was a guardian left behind by a civilization that mapped the galaxy when our ancestors were still chipping flints. It didn't want to destroy Aruna Base—it was terrified of our noise. When we stopped screaming and began to listen, the universe opened its arms."

**[EPILOGUE]**  
Aruna Station becomes the permanent seat of the UN Office of First Contact. The Entity remains in the lower borehole caverns, communicating through harmonic sound pulses and assisting human physicists in understanding gravity-acoustic resonance. A new epoch of human history begins.

### ENDING 5: "THE BURDEN" (Achievement: Haunted)
*Condition: Only Okafor (or 1 Crew Member) Survives | Data Preserved | High Personal Survival Priority*

**[VISUAL]**  
Aiden Okafor sits alone in the cavernous cargo hold of *Hermes IV*. Beside him are five sealed body pods draped in foil insulation. In his lap is the data canister, glowing softly.

He takes off his helmet. His hands are shaking. The reflection in the darkened visor shows hollow, bloodshot eyes. The radio is buzzing with enthusiastic congratulations from Houston Mission Control: *"Aiden! The world is celebrating! You did it! You brought the data home!"* 

He doesn't reply. He reaches down and clicks off the comm switch. Silence fills the cabin.

**[VOICEOVER: DR. AIDEN OKAFOR]**  
"I survived because I made the logical choices. I protected my suit. I hoarded the oxygen. I grabbed the hard drives instead of opening stasis pod 3. They gave me a medal in Washington. Parades down Broadway. But every time I close my eyes in the dark, I hear the sound of Elena's stasis pod venting. I hear Sarah asking me to hold on. The data is worth billions. But my soul stayed behind in Shackleton Crater."

**[AUDIO: HOUSTON MISSION CONTROL (DESPERATE RADIO)]**  
"Aruna Base, this is Houston Capcom. We have lost telemetry from *Hermes IV*. Trajectory calculations show the craft has initiated an uncommanded orbital burn. It’s heading toward Earth intercept. Aiden, Sarah, anyone, acknowledge... please acknowledge..."

The screen fades to black. A deep, chilling 432 Hz tone sounds, vibrating through the speakers. A single line of text appears:
*"Why did you wake what was sleeping?"*
