# 📂 ARUNA STATION: CREW DOSSIERS & CHARACTER SPECIFICATIONS
**Classification: Confidential / NASA-ISRO Joint Deep Space Initiative (2047)**
**Project Codename: ECHO PROTOCOL**

---

## 1. DR. AIDEN OKAFOR (Player Character)
- **Role:** Mission Commander / Lead Triage Specialist, Rescue Mission 1 (*Hermes IV*)
- **Age:** 39 | **Nationality:** Nigerian-British (ESA/NASA Joint Operations)
- **Background:** Former naval acoustic engineer turned astronaut. Specializes in non-optical subsurface navigation and closed-loop habitat crisis management. Calm under severe sensory deprivation.
- **Psychological Profile:** Highly analytical with deep empathetic instincts. Carries the memory of a past aborted decompression drill where he had to seal off a companion module.
- **Suit Equipment:**
  - Mark-IX EVA Heavy Exosuit (Lead-lined composite)
  - Custom Sonar Transceiver Backpack (Acoustic PING array)
  - Biometric Life-Sign HUD & Audio Spectrogram Visor
- **Gameplay Role:** The player's avatar. His voiceover reflects the player's choices and moral state throughout the expedition.

---

## 2. CAPTAIN SARAH CHEN
- **Role:** Commander, Aruna Lunar Base Expedition 04
- **Age:** 44 | **Nationality:** American (NASA)
- **Specialization:** Subsurface Geophysics & Planetary Habitation
- **Psychological Profile:** Charismatic, decisive, fiercely protective of her crew. Refused to evacuate when the deep crater anomaly was discovered, believing understanding it was humanity's destiny.
- **Narrative Arc:**
  - *Act 1:* Resolute leader trying to balance dwindling reactor power with emergency research.
  - *Act 2:* Isolated in the darkened Command Center with a motion tracker, having placed non-essential crew in stasis to conserve oxygen.
  - *Act 3:* Realizes her decision to wake the crater structure led to the deaths or freezing of her crew.
  - *Act 4:* Guides Okafor through comms: urges peaceful acoustic contact rather than violence.
- **Potential Fates:**
  - *Survivor:* Escapes on the pod, mentors future lunar explorers with quiet trauma.
  - *Heroic Fallen:* Dies holding open the reactor manual overrides.
  - *Lost in Stasis:* Found comatose if player prioritizes raw data retrieval.

## 3. DR. JAMES MURTHY
- **Role:** Chief Planetary Geologist & Xenomaterials Lead
- **Age:** 51 | **Nationality:** Indian (ISRO)
- **Specialization:** Regolith Spectrometry & Non-Terrestrial Crystalline Formations
- **Psychological Profile:** Brilliant, obsessive, academic idealist. First human to detect the structured "Meridian Signal" buried under 200m of South Pole polar ice.
- **Key Discovery:**
  - Recovered *Sample Echo-07*: An obsidian-like geometric tessellation vibrating at 432 Hz.
  - Proved that the lunar crater structures were artificially seeded over 250,000 years ago.
- **Narrative Arc:**
  - Transits from scientific elation to creeping horror as he realizes the vibration is not a passive artifact, but a beacon summoning or awakening an entity.
- **Potential Fates:**
  - *Extracted with Data:* Renowned on Earth as the father of modern Astrobiology, though forever plagued by insomnia.
  - *Killed in Lab:* Discovered slumped over the spectrometer; player must retrieve his optical keycard to unlock the telemetry terminal.

## 4. ENGINEER YUKI TANAKA
- **Role:** Chief Life Support & Reactor Systems Specialist
- **Age:** 34 | **Nationality:** Japanese (JAXA)
- **Specialization:** Tokamak Micro-Fusion & Acoustic Damping
- **Personality:** Practical, cynical, meticulous. Disagreed strongly with Captain Chen's decision to divert power to the deep lab.
- **Equipment:** Pneumatic repair wrench, arc welder, acoustic dampening ear-guards.
- **Narrative Arc:**
  - Barricaded in Engineering Deck C near the primary coolant loops.
  - Discovered that the Entity does not feed on electricity, but on the acoustic resonance frequencies of the reactor core.
- **Potential Fates:**
  - *Rescued:* Provides technical override codes to prevent the reactor meltdown during extraction.
  - *Severely Injured:* Found trapped beneath fallen conduit; player must choose whether to spend 8 minutes freeing her or race to the stasis deck.
  - *Overload Sacrifice:* Manually triggers reactor containment scram to save Okafor.

## 5. DR. KAVYA PATEL
- **Role:** Base Physician & Biometeorologist
- **Age:** 37 | **Nationality:** Indian (ISRO/UN Space Medical Board)
- **Specialization:** High-radiation surgery, hypothermic stasis protocols.
- **Personality:** Deeply compassionate, ethical anchor of the station. Refused to take a stasis pod for herself while any other crew members remained active.
- **Critical Situation:**
  - In Medical Bay Deck B, protecting the stasis bank. Her suit was punctured during the Entity's initial hull breach.
  - Currently clinging to life inside an unsealed hyperbaric pod with failing atmospheric scrubbers.
- **Gameplay Moral Dilemma:**
  - *Option A:* Use 15% of your suit's reserve oxygen to seal and replenish her chamber (costs vital survival buffer for player).
  - *Option B:* Administer sedative analgesics and prioritize extracting the research hard drive from the adjacent terminal.

## 6. SPECIALIST MARCUS VANCE & ELENA ROSTOVA (Security / EVA Recon)
- **Marcus Vance (Age 32, USSF):** Tactical systems, drone pilot. Set up automated sonic barriers that inadvertently agitated the Entity. Located in Deck B Security checkpoint.
- **Elena Rostova (Age 29, Roscosmos):** Drill operator who first pierced the sub-crater cavern.
- **Status at Rescue:** Both preserved in cryogenic stasis pods 03 & 04. Surviving their extraction depends directly on player's Resource Allocation choices in Phase 1 (Advanced Stasis Pod Couplers).

## 7. THE ENTITY: "THE MERIDIAN RESONANCE"
- **Classification:** Non-Carbon Based Acoustic-Electromagnetic Entity
- **Origin:** Sub-surface lunar mantle anomaly beneath Shackleton Crater
- **Nature & Physiology:**
  - Does not possess flesh, organs, or conventional eyes. It is a semi-coherent field of super-dense crystalline particles held together by harmonic sound waves and localized gravity fluctuations.
  - Navigates entirely by acoustic resonance and pressure waves.
  - Changes state between a diffused shadow (inaudible, imperceptible on optical cameras) and a hyper-dense, resonant crystalline hunter (shattering glass, bending steel corridors).
- **Behavioral Evolution:**
  1. *Curiosity Phase:* Mimics station machinery sounds (airlock hisses, heartbeat monitors).
  2. *Defensive Phase:* Agitated by loud concussions or sudden sonic pulses.
  3. *Hunting / Herding Phase:* Systematically herds surviving organisms toward high-resonance chambers (the Reactor).
  4. *Contact Phase:* If the player emits rhythmic, harmonic pings matching the 432 Hz Pythagorean scale, the Entity transitions from aggression to communication.
